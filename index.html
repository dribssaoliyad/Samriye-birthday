A letter from my heart

Dear Samri,

Happy birthday, my love. I wish I could put into words everything you mean to me, but even the longest letter would miss a little piece of it.

I still think about how we met while waiting for a bus in Dire Dawa. I never knew that an ordinary moment could become the beginning of something so important to me. From our first date at Dire Dawa University and Sport Cafe, to our night walks, our trip to Harar, and the moment in Debre Zeit when I asked you to be part of my forever — every memory has a place in my heart.

We are not perfect, and our story has not always been easy. Distance can be hard, but it has never made my love for you smaller. I love the way we laugh, the way ordinary places become special when we're together, and the comfort of knowing that I get to love you.

For your new year, I wish you peace, health, courage, happiness, and every beautiful thing you deserve. I hope I keep giving you reasons to smile — not only on your birthday, but on all the normal days too.

I love you so much, Samri. Today, tomorrow, and through every chapter still waiting for us.

Forever yours,
Oli ♥
